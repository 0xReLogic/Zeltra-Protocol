## Abstract

This whitepaper describes S-two, a circle STARK (Haböck, Levit, Papini 2024) over the Mersenne prime field with modulus p = 2^31 - 1. We formalize the "flat AIR" circuit model, a modern arithmetization paradigm used by several contemporary zero-knowledge virtual machines, and we provide an in-depth security analysis of our proof of proximity for flat AIRs.
