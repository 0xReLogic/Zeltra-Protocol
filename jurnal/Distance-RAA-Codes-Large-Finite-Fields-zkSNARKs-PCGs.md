## Abstract

Error-correcting codes play a central role in modern cryptography, enabling efficient constructions of primitives such as zero knowledge proofs and secure multiparty computations. Among them, repeat-accumulate-accumulate (RAA) codes have recently attracted significant attention due to their linear-time encoding and good distance properties.
