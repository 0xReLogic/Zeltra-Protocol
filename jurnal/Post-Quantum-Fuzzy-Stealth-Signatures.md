## Ringkasan

Stealth address payments improve payment privacy dan sudah deployed di beberapa cryptocurrencies. Mekanisme stealth address yang known either insecure dalam post-quantum setting atau require too much interaction. Paper ini mengusulkan post-quantum fuzzy stealth signatures yang secure terhadap quantum attacks.
