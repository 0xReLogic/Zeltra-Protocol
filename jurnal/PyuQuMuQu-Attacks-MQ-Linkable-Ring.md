**PyuQuMuQu: Attacks on MQ-Based Linkable Ring Signatures and a New 5-Pass Construction**
Freeman Slaughter

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Linkable ring signatures (LRS) let member sign on behalf of group anonymously while ensuring any two signatures by same signer are publicly linkable, major component in e-voting dan cryptocurrencies. Random systems of multivariate quadratic (MQ) equations are most conservative post-quantum assumption, yet there are only two MQ-based LRS's in literature. Paper ini presents attacks dan new 5-pass construction untuk MQ-based LRS.
