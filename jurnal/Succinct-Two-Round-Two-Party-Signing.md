**Succinct Two-Round Two-Party Signing from PCFs**
Lennart Braun, Geoffroy Couteau, Kelsey Melissaris, Mahshid Riahinia, Elahe Sadeghi

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Introduce new two-party threshold signature schemes dengan strong efficiency dan security. Application to Schnorr dan ECDSA yields two-round, two-party, stateless dan deterministic signing dengan 4-12 ms computation, extremely low communication—96 B for Schnorr, 128 B for ECDSA—dan full concurrent simulatable security. At heart is new pseudorandom correlation function construction.
