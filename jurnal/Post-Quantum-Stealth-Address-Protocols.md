## Ringkasan

Stealth Address Protocol (SAP) memungkinkan user menerima asset melalui stealth addresses yang unlinkable ke stealth meta-addresses. Dual-Key SAP (DKSAP) dan ECPDKSAP berbasis elliptic curve cryptography yang vulnerable terhadap quantum attacks. Paper ini mengusulkan post-quantum stealth address protocols.
