**zkBSA: Auditable and Compliant Stealth Addresses for Blockchains**
Siyuan Zheng, Zhe Han

**Category:** Applications

## Ringkasan

Stealth addresses berikan receiver unlinkability, tapi tidak support regulatory compliance di mana transaction amounts transparan dan recipient identities harus privat. zkBSA adalah framework modular yang menggabungkan stealth address, public-key encryption, whitelist commitment, dan ZKP untuk verify on-chain bahwa transaksi target whitelisted recipient.
