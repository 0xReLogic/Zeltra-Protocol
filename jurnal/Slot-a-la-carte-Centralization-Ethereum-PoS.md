**Slot a la carte: Centralization Issues in Ethereum's Proof-of-Stake Protocol**
János Tapolcai, Bence Ladóczki, Ábel Nagy

**Category:** Applications

In this paper, we demonstrate that Ethereum's current proof-of-stake (PoS) consensus mechanism poses a significant threat to decentralisation. Our research focuses on the manipulability of distributed randomness beacons (DRBs) in leader selection. Specifically, we show that RANDAO - Ethereum's DRB - is seriously vulnerable to manipulations in its current form.
