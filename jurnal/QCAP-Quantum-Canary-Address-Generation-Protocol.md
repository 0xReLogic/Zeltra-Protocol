## Abstract

The advent of quantum computing poses a fundamental threat to classical cryptographic assumptions. While algorithms such as RSA and Elliptic-Curve Cryptography are secure against classical adversaries, they would be efficiently broken by a sufficiently powerful quantum adversary. Yet, despite rapid industrial and academic progress, the timeline for achieving a Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer (CRQC) remains uncertain and opaque.
