**UltraMixer: A Compliant Zero-Knowledge Privacy Layer for Tokenized Real-World Assets**
Zonglun Li, Hong Kang, Xue Liu

**Category:** Applications

Real-world-asset tokens provide fractional ownership and continuous settlement but recording claims on transparent public ledgers exposes flows and positions undermining market confidentiality. UltraMixer presents a noncustodial privacy layer natively compatible with ERC-3643, enforcing compliance at boundaries via zero-knowledge proofs while enabling private asset transactions. The system reconciles privacy with enforceable regulatory access control.
