## Abstract

Decentralized Identity (DID) systems aim to provide users with self-sovereign control over their credentials. However, verifying identities on public blockchains often risks leaking sensitive attributes and transaction histories, whereas relying on heavy generic cryptographic protocols leads to prohibitive on-chain costs.
