**Computing Pairings on Elliptic Curves with Embedding Degree Two via Biextensions**
Yuhao Zheng, Jianming Lin, Chang-an Zhao

**Category:** Implementation

Bilinear pairings enable Identity-Based Encryption, short signatures, and zero-knowledge proofs. This paper optimizes pairing computations on curves with embedding degree 2 addressing both theoretical and practical implementations. An optimized double-and-add ladder algorithm leveraging y-coordinate recovery achieves superior performance for optimal ate pairing computations, enabling efficient pairing-based cryptography at 128-bit security levels.
