# Aggregator-Based Voting using proof of Partition

**Authors:** Marius Lombard-Platet, Doron Zarchy

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

**Date:** 2026-06-26

**PDF:** 2026/545

## Abstract

We present Aggios, a scalable and privacy preserving proxy voting system designed for frequent and large-scale elections such as Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAO), when storing votes on the bulletin board is expensive. To this end, Aggios introduces `aggregators': entities to which voters delegate their votes, and who then post their batched proofs on the public ledger.
