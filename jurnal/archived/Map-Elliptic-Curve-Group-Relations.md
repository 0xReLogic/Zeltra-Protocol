**Constraint-Friendly Map-to-Elliptic-Curve-Group Relations and Their Applications**
Jens Groth, Harjasleen Malvai, Andrew Miller, Yi-Nuo Zhang

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Hashing to elliptic curve groups is fundamental for multiset hashing and BLS signatures. With the rise of zero-knowledge applications, hash-to-curve constructions must work efficiently in constraint programming. Multiset hashing enables memory consistency checks in zkVMs while BLS signatures prove stake in proof-of-stake protocols. This paper presents constraint-friendly hash-to-elliptic-curve-group relations enabling practical ZK and verifiable computation applications.
