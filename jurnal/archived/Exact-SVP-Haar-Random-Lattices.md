**Exact SVP on Haar-Random Lattices at the Heuristic Sieving Exponents**
Yang Zhou, Zhedong Wang, Mingsheng Wang

**Kategori:** Fondasi

Give Monte Carlo algorithm for exact Euclidean SVP on Haar-random lattices dengan arithmetic time (3/2)^{n/2+o(n)} dan space (4/3)^{n/2+o(n)}, up to polynomial factors in logarithmic condition number. Leading exponents match those of heuristic lattice sieving. For set of lattices of Haar measure 1-o(1), algorithm succeeds dengan probability 1-o(1) on every input basis.
