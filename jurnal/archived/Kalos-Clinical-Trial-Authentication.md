**Kalos: Hierarchical-auditable and Human-binding Authentication Scheme for Clinical Trial**
Chang Chen, Zelong Wu, Guoyu Yang, Qi Chen, Wei Wang, Jin Li

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci Publik

Clinical trials crucial untuk pengembangan new medical treatment methods. Medical institutes perlu collect dan process large volumes of participant data untuk ensure correctness of results, prompting research on privacy preservation dan data reliability. Existing solutions struggle to resolve trade-off due to trust gap between physical dan digital worlds. Kalos presented untuk tackle issues ini.
