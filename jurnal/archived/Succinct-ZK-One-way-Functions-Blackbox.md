**Succinct Zero-knowledge Proofs from One-way Functions: The Blackbox Way**
Eden Florentz-Konopnicki, Ron D. Rothblum

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

This work focuses on zero-knowledge proofs that are statistically sound and computationally zero-knowledge, building on the seminal Goldreich-Micali-Wigderson result. The paper develops blackbox constructions of succinct zero-knowledge proofs from one-way functions, advancing fundamental understanding of what cryptographic primitives suffice for practical succinct proof systems.
