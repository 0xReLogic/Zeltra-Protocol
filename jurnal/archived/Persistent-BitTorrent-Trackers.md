**Persistent BitTorrent Trackers**
François-Xavier Wicht, Zhengwei Tong, Shunfan Zhou, Hang Yin, Aviv Yaish

**Category:** Applications

Private BitTorrent trackers enforce upload-to-download ratios but suffer from critical weaknesses: reputation cannot transfer between trackers, centralized servers create single points of failure, and upload statistics are self-reported and unverifiable. When trackers shut down, users lose contribution history. This paper solves these problems by storing reputation in smart contracts and replacing self-reports with verifiable mechanisms, enabling persistent, decentralized reputation across BitTorrent communities.
