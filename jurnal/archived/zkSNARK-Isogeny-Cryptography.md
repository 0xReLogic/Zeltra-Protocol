# Toward zkSNARK-assisted Isogeny-based Cryptography

**Authors:** Yi-Fu Lai, Luciano Maino  
**Published:** 2026-05-27  
**Category:** Public-key cryptography  

## Summary
Zero-knowledge proofs are a fundamental building block of modern privacy-preserving systems. In isogeny-based cryptography, existing zero-knowledge proof constructions are either limited to chains of small-degree isogenies or are quite inefficient. As a result, many relations used in recent cryptosystems lack support in generic proof systems. In this work, we take a step toward making zkSNARKs practically usable for a broader set of isogeny relations beyond the classical isogeny path...
