**On Canonical Liftings, Group Actions, and Linkable Ring Signatures**
Alessandro Budroni, Giuseppe D'Alconzo, Leonardo Errati, Edoardo Persichetti

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci-Publik

Paper ini introduces notion of canonical lifting for group actions. This generalises existing techniques for reducing size of group-action-based signatures. Building on this framework, construct Sigma protocols dan linkable ring signature scheme. Notably, approach avoids key-reuse mechanism for linkability used in framework of Beullens et al. (ASIACRYPT'20) yang was shown to be insecure for group actions.
