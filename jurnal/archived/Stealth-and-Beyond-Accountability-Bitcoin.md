**Stealth and Beyond: Attribute-Driven Accountability in Bitcoin Transactions**
Alberto Maria Mongardini, Daniele Friolo, Giuseppe Ateniese

**Category:** Public-key cryptography

## Ringkasan

Bitcoin enable decentralized, pseudonymous transactions, tapi balance privacy dengan accountability tetap tantangan. Paper ini introduce dual accountability mechanism yang enforce sender dan recipient compliance. Senders restricted spending UTXOs yang meet criteria tertentu, recipients harus satisfy legal/ethical requirements sebelum terima funds.
