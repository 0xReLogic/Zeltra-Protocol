**Privacy-Preserving Identifier Checking in 5G**
Marcel D.S.K. Gräfenstein, Stefan Köpsell, Maryam Zarezadeh

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Device identifiers seperti International Mobile Equipment Identity (IMEI) crucial untuk ensuring device integrity dan meeting regulations di 4G dan 5G networks. Namun sharing identifiers dengan Mobile Network Operators (MNOs) brings significant privacy risks enabling long-term tracking dan linking of user activities across sessions. Paper ini proposes privacy-preserving identifier checking method di 5G dengan protocol untuk verifying device identifiers tanpa expose privacy.
