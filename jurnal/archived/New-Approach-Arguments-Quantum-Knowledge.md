**A New Approach to Arguments of Quantum Knowledge**
James Bartusek, Ruta Jawale, Justin Raizes, Kabir Tomer

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

This work constructs a publicly-verifiable non-interactive zero-knowledge argument system for QMA with transparent setup (URS only) and extractability properties. Unlike prior work requiring obfuscated programs as CRS, the protocol achieves these properties with minimal setup assumptions. Valid QMA witnesses can be extracted directly from accepting proofs, enabling practical quantum knowledge attestation suitable for quantum computing applications.
