## Abstract

We consider the worst-case hardness of the gap version of the classic time-bounded Kolmogorov complexity problem—Gap_pMK^tP[s_1,s_2]—where the goal is to determine whether for a given string x, K^t(x) ≤ s_1(n) or K^{p(t)}(x) > s_2(n), where K^t(x) denotes the t-bounded Kolmogorov complexity of x.
