## Ringkasan

Mimblewimble adalah cryptocurrency protocol yang promises overcome scalability issues dan provide user privacy. Lack of non-interactive transactions sudah lama hinder adoption. Yu proposed cara menambahkan non-interactive transactions ke stealth addresses di Mimblewimble, tapi flawed. Paper ini give fixed version.
