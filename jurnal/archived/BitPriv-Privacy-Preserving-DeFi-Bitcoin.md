## Ringkasan

Bitcoin secures over a trillion dollars, tapi scripting language limits expressive DeFi applications. BitVM enables arbitrary off-chain computation, tapi tidak provide privacy. BitPriv adalah protocol yang conditions payments pada outcome of secure two-party computation over private inputs on unmodified Bitcoin.
