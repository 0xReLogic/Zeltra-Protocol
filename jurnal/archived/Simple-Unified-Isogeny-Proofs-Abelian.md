# A Simple and Unified Approach for Proving Knowledge of Isogenies between Abelian Varieties

**Authors:** Jonathan Komada Eriksen, Riccardo Invernizzi, Jannik Spiessens, Frederik Vercauteren  
**Published:** 2026-06-01  
**Category:** Public-key cryptography  

## Summary
In this paper we introduce a simple and unified approach, based on generic proof systems, to prove knowledge of any isogeny between two principally polarized abelian varieties in any dimension, assuming that the $2^m$-torsion is accessible for sufficiently large $m$.
