**Applications Of Zero-Knowledge Proofs On Bitcoin**
Yusuf Ozmiş

**Category:** Applications

This paper explores how zero-knowledge proofs enhance Bitcoin's functionality and privacy. Proof-of-Reserve schemes use zk-STARKs to prove custodian holdings exceed threshold X without revealing addresses or balances. ZK Light Clients enable mobile/lightweight devices to verify Bitcoin's proof-of-work chain efficiently. Both applications improve Bitcoin's privacy and scalability without protocol changes.
