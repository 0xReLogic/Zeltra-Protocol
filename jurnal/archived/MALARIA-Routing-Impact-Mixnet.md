**MALARIA: Management of Low-Latency Routing Impact on Mix Network Anonymity**
Mahdi Rahimi

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Mix networks offer robust anonymity even against adversaries monitoring all network links, namun impose high latency on communications. To address this, recent research explores strategic low-latency routing within mixnets. While these strategies appear to reduce latency, their impact on mixnet anonymity tidak carefully assessed. Paper ini quantifies anonymity loss dari routing strategies dan addresses potential deanonymization of clients.
