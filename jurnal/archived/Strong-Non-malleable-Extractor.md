**Efficient randomized strong 2-source non-malleable extractor for any linear min-entropy**
Divesh Aggarwal, Pranjal Dutta, Saswata Mukherjee, Satyajeet Nagargoje, Maciej Obremski

**Category:** Foundations

Randomness is fundamental for cryptographic systems enabling secure encryption, commitments, and zero-knowledge proofs. Real-world sources suffer from weaknesses exploitable by adversaries. While deterministic extraction from single sources is impossible, two-source extractors generate nearly uniform randomness from two independent weak sources. This paper presents efficient randomized strong 2-source non-malleable extractors working for any linear min-entropy.
