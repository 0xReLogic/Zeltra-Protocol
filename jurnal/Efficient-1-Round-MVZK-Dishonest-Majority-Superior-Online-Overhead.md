## Abstract

Zero-knowledge proofs allow to prove a statement while not revealing anything about the witness. In particular, non-interactive zero-knowledge (NIZK) has emerged as the preferred paradigm due to its public verifiability. However, existing NIZK protocols often suffer from high prover overhead for large statements.
