**Foundations of Ring Sampling**
Viktoria Ronge, Christoph Egger, Russell W. F. Lai, Dominique Schröder, Hoover H. F. Yin

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Ring signature scheme allows signer to sign on behalf of ad hoc set of users called ring. Verifier can be convinced that ring member signs, but cannot point to exact signer. Ring signatures have become increasingly important with deployment in anonymous cryptocurrencies. Conventionally assumed all ring members are equally likely to be signer. This assumption generally false in reality, leading to practical dan theoretical issues.
