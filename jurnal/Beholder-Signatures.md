**Beholder Signatures**
Stefan Dziembowski, Sebastian Faust, Paweł Kędzior, Marcin Mielniczuk, Susil Kumar Mohanty, Krzysztof Pietrzak

**Category:** Public-key cryptography

This work introduces beholder signatures, the opposite of blind signatures. One signs a commitment to a (potentially very long) message, and the signature attests that parties participating in signing who know the secret key also know the entire committed message. This guarantee holds against distributed adversaries using secure multiparty computation, enabling novel authentication scenarios where signers prove knowledge of committed values without revealing them.
