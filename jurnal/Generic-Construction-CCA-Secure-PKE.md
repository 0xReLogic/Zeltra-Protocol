**Generic Construction of CCA-Secure PKE from Key-Insulated and Privacy-Preserving Signatures with Publicly Derived Public Key**
Ryo Mizuno, Keita Emura

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

---

## Ringkasan

Liu et al. (EuroS&P 2019) mengusulkan Key-Insulated and Privacy-Preserving Signature Scheme with Publicly Derived Public Keys (PDPKS) untuk enhance security stealth addresses. Di scheme ini, payee generate master public/secret key pair, dan payer derive public key dari payee's master public key. Paper ini menyediakan generic construction untuk CCA-secure PKE dari PDPKS.
