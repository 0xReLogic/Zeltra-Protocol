**Secure Hierarchical Deterministic Wallet Supporting Stealth Address**
Xin Yin, Zhen Liu, Guomin Yang, Guoxing Chen, Haojin Zhu

**Category:** Public-key cryptography

## Ringkasan

Digital wallet adalah primary entrance untuk akses cryptocurrency assets. Hierarchical Deterministic Wallet (HDW) dari BIP32 widely used karena easy backup/recovery, convenient cold-address management, dan trust-less operation. Paper ini mengusulkan secure HDW yang support stealth address.
