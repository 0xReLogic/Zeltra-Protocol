**Unique NIZKs and Steganography Detection**
Willy Quach, LaKyah Tyner, Daniel Wichs

**Category:** Foundations

Non-interactive zero-knowledge proofs tend to be randomized with many possible proofs per statement. Can NIZKs have only a single unique valid proof per statement? Such NIZKs are known under strong assumptions (indistinguishability obfuscation) but require strong assumptions conversely (witness encryption). This paper explores relaxed notions of unique NIZKs and their applications to steganography detection, advancing fundamental understanding of proof uniqueness and its cryptographic implications.
