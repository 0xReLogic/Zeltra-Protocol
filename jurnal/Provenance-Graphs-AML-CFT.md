**Provenance Graphs for AML/CFT: Asset Tracing and Freezing in Private Payments**
Maxence Brugeres, Petr Kuznetsov

**Category:** Applications

---

## Ringkasan

AML/CFT rules require payment service providers untuk assess incoming funds' provenance. Di transparent ledgers seperti Bitcoin, ini reduce ke graph traversal. Di private payment systems seperti Zerocash, coins di-burn dan created di setiap pembayaran. Paper ini membahas bagaimana melakukan asset tracing dan freezing di private payments.
