## Abstract

We revisit Group Encryption (GE)—an encryption analogue of group signatures introduced by Kiayias et al. (Asiacrypt 2007). A GE system simultaneously provides anonymity and traceability for receivers who are certified group members, enabling a range of privacy-preserving applications.
