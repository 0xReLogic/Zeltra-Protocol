**Higher-order differential attacks on the full DuX**
Guoqiang Liu, Bing Sun

**Kategori:** Serangan dan Kriptanalisis

DuX adalah family of substitution-permutation block ciphers over F_q^{16} with q in {2^8, 2^16, 65537} dan twelve rounds. Designers estimate integral dan higher-order differential distinguishers in encryption direction reach at most six rounds. Paper ini bounds word-wise algebraic degree of DuX by exponent sets in decryption direction, enabling higher-order differential attacks on full DuX.
