**Introducing two ROS attack variants: breaking one-more unforgeability of BZ blind signatures**
Bruno M. F. Ricardo, Lucas C. Cardoso, Leonardo T. Kimura, Paulo S. Barreto, Marcos A. Simplicio Jr

**Category:** Attacks and cryptanalysis

In 2023, Barreto and Zanon proposed three-round Schnorr-like blind signature scheme BZ using zero-knowledge proofs to produce one-time signatures. BZ claimed resistance to Random inhomogeneities in Overdetermined Solvable systems of linear equations modulo prime (ROS) attacks. This paper introduces two ROS attack variants breaking BZ's one-more unforgeability, demonstrating practical vulnerabilities in the proposed scheme.
