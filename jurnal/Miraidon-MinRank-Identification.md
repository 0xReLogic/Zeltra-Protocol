**Miraidon: MinRank Identification**
Ryann Cartor, Freeman Slaughter

**Kategori:** Protokol Kryptografi

Miraidon is new family of MinRank-based post-quantum signature schemes built from novel zero-knowledge proof system. Primary construction, Miraidon-S, is digital signature scheme dengan competitive public key dan signature sizes, improved soundness parameters, dan security based on hardness of MinRank problem. Building on this framework, further construct Miraidon-RS, a ring signature scheme dengan enhanced properties.
