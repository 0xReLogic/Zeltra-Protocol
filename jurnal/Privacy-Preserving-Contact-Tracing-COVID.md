**Privacy-Preserving Contact Tracing of COVID-19 Patients**
Leonie Reichert, Samuel Brack, Björn Scheuermann

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Current Covid-19 pandemic shows modern globalized world can be heavily affected by quickly spreading, highly infectious, deadly virus dalam matter of weeks. Manual contact tracing dan quarantining of suspects can only be effective in first days before exponential growth overwhelms health authorities. By automating tracing processes dan quarantining everyone who came in contact with infected patients, privacy-preserving contact tracing systems address pandemic response.
