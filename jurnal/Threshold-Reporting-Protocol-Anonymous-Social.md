**Threshold Reporting Protocol for Traceability in Anonymous Social Networks**
Olivier Blazy, Lola-Baie Mallordy

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Anonymous social networks continue to grow in popularity, raise serious challenges around abuse, misinformation, dan harmful behavior. Traditional de-anonymization techniques can address misuse but often come at cost of user privacy. Protecting honest users' privacy while keeping malicious ones accountable remains complex challenge. Conditional deanonymization adalah promising alternative dimana anonymity lifted only when abuse is collectively reported dan verified.
