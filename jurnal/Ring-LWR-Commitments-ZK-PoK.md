**Ring-LWR based Commitments and ZK-PoKs with Application to Verifiable Quantum-Safe Searchable Symmetric Encryption**
Debadrita Talapatra, Nimish Mishra, Debdeep Mukhopadhyay

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Prior lattice-based ZK proofs mostly rely on Learning-With-Errors assumption. This paper proposes zero-knowledge proofs using Ring Learning with Rounding assumption for linear polynomial relations. The first efficient RLWR-based commitment scheme enables verifiable quantum-safe searchable symmetric encryption applications.
