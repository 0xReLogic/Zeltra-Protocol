**Mixing Confidential Transactions: Comprehensive Transaction Privacy for Bitcoin**
Tim Ruffing, Pedro Moreno-Sanchez

**Category:** Applications

## Ringkasan

Public nature of blockchain adalah severe threat untuk privacy Bitcoin users. Funds bisa tracked dan tainted, no two coins equal, fungibility at risk. Beberapa privacy-enhancing technologies sudah proposed, tapi either require deep redesign of currency. Paper ini mengusulkan mixing confidential transactions untuk comprehensive transaction privacy.
