# Couplet: Multivariate Polynomial Commitments with Two-Group-Element Openings

**Authors:** Weijie Wang, Alin Tomescu, Rex Fernando, Charalampos Papamanthou  
**Published:** 2026-09-19  
**Category:** Cryptographic protocols  

## Summary
We present Couplet, a new pairing-based multivariate polynomial commitment scheme (PCS). Couplet is the first multivariate PCS whose opening proofs are solely two group elements. In addition, for the case of multilinear polynomials, Couplet is the first constant-proof PCS with a prover that requires only a sublinear number of group operations.
