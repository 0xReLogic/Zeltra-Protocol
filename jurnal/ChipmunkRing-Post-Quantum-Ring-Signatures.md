**ChipmunkRing: A Practical Post-Quantum Ring Signature Scheme for Blockchain Applications**
Dmitrii A. Gerasimov

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

ChipmunkRing presents a practical post-quantum ring signature construction for blockchain environments. Built on the Chipmunk lattice-based cryptographic framework, it delivers compact signatures (20.5-279.7KB) with rapid signing (1.1-15.1ms) and efficient validation (0.4-4.5ms) for groups of 2-64 members. Acorn Verification streamlines the approach, enabling practical post-quantum ring signatures for decentralized systems.
