# ZK-JPEG: Zero-knowledge Image Editing and Compression

**Authors:** Samuel Dittmer, Steve Lu, Kimberlee Model, Joseph Near  
**Published:** 2026-09-15  
**Category:** Applications  

## Summary
Tools for generating deep fake photographs are proliferating with greater ease of use and prominence in pop culture. Image authentication tools can defeat these deceitful developments by verifying that a digital image was actually produced by a physical camera. The challenge is that these tools must be robust to desirable image transformations. Camera attestation uses digital signatures to prove an image's provenance from a camera.
