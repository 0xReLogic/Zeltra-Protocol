**Threshold BBS+ Signatures for Distributed Anonymous Credential Issuance**
Jack Doerner, Yashvanth Kondi, Eysa Lee, abhi shelat, LaKyah Tyner

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Paper ini propose secure multiparty signing protocol untuk BBS+ signature scheme, i.e., anonymous credential scheme dengan threshold issuance. Prove bahwa due to structure of BBS+ signature, simply verifying signature produced by otherwise semi-honest protocol is sufficient untuk achieve composable security against malicious adversary. Protocol extremely simple dan efficient: involves single request from client who requires signature.
