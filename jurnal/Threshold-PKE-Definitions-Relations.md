**Threshold Public-Key Encryption: Definitions, Relations, and CPA-to-CCA Transforms**
Chris Brzuska, Michael Klooß, Ivy K. Y. Woo

**Category:** Public-key cryptography

Threshold public-key encryption (TPKE) allows t out of k parties to jointly decrypt ciphertexts while ensuring confidentiality against coalitions of t-1 parties. Despite its long history and standardization efforts, basic security notions lack dedicated study and variations differ. This paper initiates systematic TPKE study, develops relations between indistinguishability notions, and provides CPA-to-CCA transforms for practical TPKE schemes.
