**Inner-Product Commitments Over Integers With Applications to Succinct Arguments**
Shihui Fu

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Proving statements over integers is crucial for modern cryptographic protocols as certain computations like range proofs and Diophantine satisfiability are more efficiently expressed over integers. Current approaches convert integer relations to statements over finite fields but finding corresponding tractable statements is not always straightforward. This paper develops inner-product commitments over integers, enabling efficient proofs for integer-based statements and succinct arguments.
