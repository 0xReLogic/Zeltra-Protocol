**Keyed-Verification Anonymous Credentials with Highly Efficient Partial Disclosure**
Omid Mirzamohammadi, Jan Bobolz, Mahdi Sedaghat, Emad Heydari Beni, Aysajan Abidin, Dave Singelee, Bart Preneel

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Anonymous credential (AC) system dengan partial disclosure memungkinkan users prove possession of credential issued by issuer while selectively disclosing subset of attributes in privacy-preserving manner. Di keyed-verification AC (KVAC) systems, issuer dan verifier share secret key. Existing KVAC schemes rely on computationally expensive zero-knowledge proofs during credential presentation dengan presentation size growing linearly.
