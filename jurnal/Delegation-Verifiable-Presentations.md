**On Delegation of Verifiable Presentations from mdoc and BBS Credentials**
Andrea Flamini, Andrea Gangemi, Enrico Guglielmino, Vincenzo Orabona

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Interest dalam verifiable credential systems telah gained traction as eIDAS 2.0 Regulation published. Regulation instructs EU member states to provide citizens dengan digital identity wallets (EUDI Wallet) yang must store credentials dan enable privacy-preserving presentation of identity information to relying parties. Paper ini defines protocols dan procedures untuk delegation of verifiable presentations.
