**Efficiently parsing existing eID documents for zero-knowledge proofs**
Tom Godden, Ruben De Smet, Kris Steenhaut, An Braeken

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Online services increasingly require users to verify identity atau parts of it, often by law. Verification biasanya dilakukan dengan processing data dari official identity documents seperti national identity cards. Tetapi documents ini contains significantly more information than verifying party needs, termasuk information yang harus private. Paper ini presents efficient parsing methods untuk existing eID documents enabling ZK proofs.
