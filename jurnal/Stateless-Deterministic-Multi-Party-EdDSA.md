**Stateless Deterministic Multi-Party EdDSA Signatures with Low Communication**
Qi Feng, Kang Yang, Kaiyi Zhang, Xiao Wang, Yu Yu, Xiang Xie

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

EdDSA is standardized signing algorithm, by IRTF dan NIST, widely used in blockchain seperti Hyperledger, Cardano, Zcash. It is variant of Schnorr signature leveraging Edwards curves, features stateless dan deterministic nonce generation tidak rely on reliable source of randomness atau state continuity. Paper ini presents stateless deterministic multi-party EdDSA signatures dengan low communication.
