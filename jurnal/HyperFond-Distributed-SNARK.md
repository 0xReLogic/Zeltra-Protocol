**HyperFond: A Transparent and Post-Quantum Distributed SNARK with Polylogarithmic Communication**
Yuanzhuo Yu, Mengling Liu, Yuncong Zhang, Shi-Feng Sun, Tianyi Ma, Man Ho Au, Dawu Gu

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Distributed SNARKs accelerate proof generation by distributing workload across machines. However, most existing schemes require trusted setups or rely on quantum-vulnerable assumptions. HyperFond presents a transparent, post-quantum secure distributed SNARK with polylogarithmic communication complexity, enabling scalable proving without trusted setup while resisting quantum attacks.
