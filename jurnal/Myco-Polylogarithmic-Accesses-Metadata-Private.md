**Myco: Unlocking Polylogarithmic Accesses in Metadata-Private Messaging**
Darya Kaviani, Deevashwer Rathee, Bhargav Annem, Raluca Ada Popa

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Billions rely on end-to-end encrypted messaging, exposure of metadata seperti communication timing dan participant relationships continues to deanonymize users. Asynchronous metadata-hiding solutions with strong cryptographic guarantees historically bottlenecked by quadratic O(N²) server computation due to reliance on private information retrieval (PIR). Myco is metadata-private messaging system that preserves strong anonymity dengan polylogarithmic accesses.
