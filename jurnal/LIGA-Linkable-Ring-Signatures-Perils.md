**A note on the Unsuitability of LIGA for Linkable Ring Signatures: The perils of non-commutativity**
Iñigo Diaz Iribarnegaray, Václav Gregor, Florian L'écu Leal

**Kategori:** Serangan dan Kriptanalisis

Paper ini studies proposal untuk linkable ring signature (LRS) instantiated dari group action framework using Lattice Isomorphism Group Action (LIGA), where security rests on Lattice Isomorphism Problem (LIP). Show bahwa signature does not fit requirements untuk be linkable ring signature, despite guarantees of BKP20 framework, due to it straying from required structural properties.
