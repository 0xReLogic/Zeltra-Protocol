**Making BBS Anonymous Credentials eIDAS 2.0 Compliant**
Nicolas Desmoulins, Antoine Dumanois, Seyni Kane, Jacques Traoré

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

eIDAS 2.0 adalah ambitious regulation aimed at equipping European citizens dengan personal digital identity wallet (EU Digital Identity Wallet) on mobile phone. Needs high level of security, available untuk large number of citizens, dan respect privacy (GDPR). Paper ini introduce foundations untuk making BBS anonymous credentials eIDAS 2.0 compliant.
