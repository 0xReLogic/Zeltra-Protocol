**Practical Key Recovery Attacks on Full DuX and Reduced-Round YuX**
Xingwei Ren, Bo Xu, Zhenyu Xiong, Yongqiang Li, Xichao Hu, Lin Jiao, Mingsheng Wang

**Kategori:** Serangan dan Kriptanalisis

DuX dan YuX are recent families of FHE-friendly block ciphers designed for efficient evaluation under fully homomorphic encryption. Paper ini shows that, in chosen-ciphertext model, algebraic degree of their decryption functions grows far more slowly than designers' evaluation of encryption direction, enabling practical key recovery attacks.
