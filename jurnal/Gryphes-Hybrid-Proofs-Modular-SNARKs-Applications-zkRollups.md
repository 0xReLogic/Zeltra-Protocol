**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

## Abstract

We address the challenge of constructing a proof system capable of handling multiple computations that involve diverse types of tasks, such as scalable zkRollup applications. A central dilemma in this design is the trade-off between generality and efficiency: while arithmetic circuit-based SNARKs offer fast proofs but limited flexibility, zkVMs provide general-purpose programmability at the cost of considerable overhead for circuit translation.
