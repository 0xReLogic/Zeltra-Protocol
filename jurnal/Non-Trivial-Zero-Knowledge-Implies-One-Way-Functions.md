## Abstract

A recent breakthrough [Hirahara and Nanashima, STOC'2024] established that if NP ⊄ ioP/poly, the existence of zero-knowledge (ZK) with negligible errors for NP implies the existence of one-way functions (OWFs). This work obtains a characterization of one-way functions from the worst-case complexity of zero-knowledge in the high-error regime.
