**SUMMER: Recursive Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Scalable RNN Training**
Yuange Li, Xiong Fan

**Category:** Applications

Zero-knowledge proofs of training enable provers to certify model training on committed datasets under prescribed algorithms without revealing model or data. Proving RNN training is challenging due to hidden-state recurrence, cross-step weight sharing requiring proofs of recurrence, gradients, and nonlinear activations. SUMMER presents a recursive zkPoT for scalable RNNs combining sumcheck and Merkle trees, enabling efficient verifiable RNN training.
