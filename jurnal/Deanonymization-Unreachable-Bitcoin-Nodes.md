**A New Approach to Deanonymization of Unreachable Bitcoin Nodes**
Indra Deep Mastan, Souradyuti Paul

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Mounting deanonymization attacks on unreachable Bitcoin nodes residing behind NAT adalah challenging task. Such attack first given by Biryukov, Khovratovich dan Pustogarov based on observation bahwa node can be uniquely identified in single session by directly-connected neighbouring nodes. BKP15 attack less effective across multiple sessions. Paper ini addresses this issue dengan new approach untuk deanonymization.
