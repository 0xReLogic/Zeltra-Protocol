**MinMandate: Private Task-Scoped Payment Authorization for Adaptive Agent Workflows**
Ge Gao, Haining Yu, Zhichao Liu, Dongyang Zhan, Yuanxiao Zhu, Zhongyun Hua

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

---

## Ringkasan

Autonomous agents semakin banyak digunakan untuk plan dan execute paid workflows. Existing agentic-payment frameworks require user to specify merchants sebelum execution. MinMandate mengusulkan private task-scoped payment authorization di mana agents bisa choose among merchants berdasarkan intermediate results.
