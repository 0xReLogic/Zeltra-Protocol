## Abstract

Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-interactive Arguments of Knowledge (zkSNARKs) are important cryptographic primitives critical in many real-world applications. zkSNARKs are not used in isolation but are deployed within a broader context in which other cryptographic protocols may be concurrently executed.
