**d/v-CLSAG: Extension for Concise Linkable Spontaneous Anonymous Group Signatures**
sowle

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Paper ini presents Schnorr-like linkable ring signature scheme called d/v-CLSAG yang is extension for d-CLSAG scheme. Proposed extension allows use of different group generators for different layers of ring members, while original scheme assumes same generator G across all layers. Provide security statements untuk updated scheme.
