**Analysis of DP3T**
Serge Vaudenay

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

To help fighting COVID-19 pandemic, Pan-European Privacy-Preserving Proximity Tracing (PEPP-PT) project proposed Decentralized Privacy-Preserving Proximity Tracing (DP3T) system. This helps tracking spread of SARS-CoV-2 virus while keeping privacy of individuals safe. In this report, analyze security dan privacy protection of DP3T. Without questioning how effective it could be against pandemic, show that it may introduce severe risks to society.
