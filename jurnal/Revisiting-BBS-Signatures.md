**Revisiting BBS Signatures**
Stefano Tessaro, Chenzhi Zhu

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci Publik

BBS signatures were implicitly proposed by Boneh, Boyen, dan Shacham (CRYPTO '04) sebagai part of their group signature scheme, dan explicitly cast as stand-alone signatures by Camenisch dan Lysyanskaya (CRYPTO '04). Provably secure version called BBS+ devised by Au, Susilo, dan Mu (SCN '06), currently object of standardization effort yang led ke recent RFC draft. BBS+ signatures suitable untuk anonymous credential dan DAA systems.
