**Privacy-Preserving Identity Management and Software Bill of Materials Vulnerability Detection: Practical Use Cases from the PRIVIDEMA Project**
Mariya Georgieva Belorgey, Benoit Cogliati, Simon Demarty, Lois Huguenin-Dumittan, Özcan Öztürk, Salma Rasti Samiei, Oana Stan

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

PRIVIDEMA project advances state of art dalam cryptographic dan Privacy-Enhancing Technologies untuk enable secure, interoperable, dan trustworthy data exchange across sectors, with focus pada Cyber Threat Intelligence dan Digital Identity Management. Paper ini presents two representative real-world use-cases: identity management for digital wallets dan secure data sharing untuk cyber threat intelligence.
