**Non-Interactive Blind Signatures from RSA Assumption and More**
Lucjan Hanzlik, Eugenio Paracucchi, Riccardo Zanotto

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci-Publik

Blind signatures received increased attention. They allow users to obtain signature under message without revealing it to signer. Most popular application is one-time tokens, dimana issuing is not linkable to redeeming phase, dan signature under random identifier forms valid token. This concept is backbone of Privacy Pass system. Paper ini presents non-interactive blind signatures dari RSA assumption.
