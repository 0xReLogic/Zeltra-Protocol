**Incremental Keyword Private Information Retrieval from d-ary Segmented Cuckoo Filters**
Bao Ninh

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

RisePIR introduces first preprocessing keyword PIR scheme that absorbs insert, update, dan delete on key-value store at cost proportional to number of mutations alone. Where static state-of-the-art schemes ChalametPIR dan KPIR-index re-run preprocessing from scratch on every change, RisePIR patches preprocessing in place dan stays as practical as both in online phase.
