**COMMON: Order Book with Privacy**
Albert Garreta, Adam Gągol, Aikaterini-Panagiota Stouka, Damian Straszak, Michal Zajac

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Decentralized Finance (DeFi) telah menyaksikan remarkable growth dan innovation, dengan Decentralized Exchanges (DEXes) playing pivotal role dalam ecosystem ini. numerous DEX designs emerge, challenges seperti price inefficiency dan lack of user privacy continue to prevail. Paper ini introduces novel DEX design called COMMON yang addresses kedua challenges. COMMON operates sebagai order book, natively integrated dengan shielded token pool, providing anonymity kepada traders.
