**Multivariate Commitments and Signatures with Efficient Protocols**
Charles Bouillaguet, Thibauld Feneuil, Jules Maire, Matthieu Rivain, Julia Sauvage, Damien Vergnaud

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

This work revisits multivariate commitments based on the hardness of solving multivariate quadratic (MQ) equations. The construction commits message μ as c = (μ + F(r), G(r)) where F and G are random quadratic maps. The paper proves computational hiding under MQ intractability and shows binding reduces to solving random bilinear systems, which is NP-complete. Performance analysis demonstrates practical viability of post-quantum multivariate commitment schemes.
