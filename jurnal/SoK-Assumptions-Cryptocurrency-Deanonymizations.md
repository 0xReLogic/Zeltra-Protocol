**SoK: Assumptions Underlying Cryptocurrency Deanonymizations -- A Taxonomy for Scientific Experts and Legal Practitioners**
Dominic Deuber, Viktoria Ronge, Christian Rückert

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci-Secara

Cryptocurrencies increasingly used in cybercrime dan become key payment method di darknet marketplaces, partly due to alleged anonymity. Research attacking anonymity of even cryptocurrencies that claim to offer anonymity by design is growing dan applied by law enforcement agencies. Investigative measures require certain degree of suspicion dan it is unclear whether findings dari deanonymization attacks are scientifically valid.
