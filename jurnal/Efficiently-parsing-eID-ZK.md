**Efficiently parsing existing eID documents for zero-knowledge proofs**
Tom Godden, Ruben De Smet, Kris Steenhaut, An Braeken

**Category:** Applications

Online services increasingly require identity verification by law. Processing official eID documents often reveals more information than needed, including private data. This poses privacy and security risks. This paper presents efficient parsing methods for existing eID documents enabling zero-knowledge proofs that disclose only required attributes while preserving privacy of sensitive information.
