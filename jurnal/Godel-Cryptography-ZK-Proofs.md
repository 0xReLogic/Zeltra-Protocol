**Gödel in Cryptography: Effectively Zero-Knowledge Proofs for NP with No Interaction, No Setup, and Perfect Soundness**
Rahul Ilango

**Category:** Foundations

Zero-knowledge proofs demonstrate facts are true without revealing solutions. Classical impossibility by Goldreich and Oren shows ZK proofs must sacrifice basic properties like perfect soundness. This paper presents effectively zero-knowledge proofs for NP with no interaction, no setup, and perfect soundness, achieving what was previously thought impossible through novel proof system design.
