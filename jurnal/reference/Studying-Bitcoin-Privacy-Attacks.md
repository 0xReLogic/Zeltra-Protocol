**Studying Bitcoin privacy attacks and their Impact on Bitcoin-based Identity Methods**
Simin Ghesmati, Walid Fdhila, Edgar Weippl

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Bitcoin blockchain adalah first publicly verifiable dan distributed ledger where everyone can download dan check full history of all data records dari genesis block. These properties lead to emergence of new applications dan redesign of traditional systems. One particular application adalah use of blockchain technology untuk identity methods. Paper ini studies Bitcoin privacy attacks dan their impact on Bitcoin-based identity methods.
