**SoK: How private is Bitcoin? Classification and Evaluation of Bitcoin Mixing Techniques**
Simin Ghesmati, Walid Fdhila, Edgar Weippl

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Blockchain is disruptive technology yang promises multitude of benefits seperti transparency, traceability, dan immutability. However, this bundle of key characteristics rapidly proved to be double-edged sword yang can put user privacy at risk. Unlike traditional systems, Bitcoin transactions are publicly dan permanently recorded, anyone can access full history. Despite using pseudonymous identities, adversary can undermine financial privacy.
