## Ringkasan

Meskipun ada advances dalam high-assurance verified implementations, implementasinya mengalami performance overhead karena formal verification dan automated extraction. Paper ini address performance challenges dengan presenting formal treatment untuk accelerate verified implementations berbasis multiple optimizations.
