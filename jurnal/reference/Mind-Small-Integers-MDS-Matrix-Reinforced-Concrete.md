# Mind Small Integers in MDS Matrix: Collision Attacks on Round-Reduced Reinforced Concrete

**Authors:** Jiamin Cui, Fukang Liu, Jianqiang Ni, Willi Meier  
**Published:** 2026-09-17  
**Category:** Attacks and cryptanalysis  

## Summary
The rapid advancement of Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP) has motivated the design of ZK-friendly hash functions. A relatively new design strategy for ZK-friendly hash functions is using the composition of small look-up tables (LUTs) to build a nonlinear transform (called \bars layer) over a large prime field $\Fp$.
