**Category:** Secret-key cryptography

Zero-Knowledge (ZK) proof systems have become a cornerstone of privacy-preserving technologies and blockchain scalability. However, traditional hash functions are often inefficient within ZK protocols due to their high constraint complexity in arithmetic circuits.
