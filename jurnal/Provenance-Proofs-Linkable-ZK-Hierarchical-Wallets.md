**Provenance Proofs: Linkable Zero-Knowledge Derivation Relations for Hierarchical Deterministic Wallets**
Vincenzo Botta, Michal Pospieszalski, Emanuele Ragnoli, Justus Ranvier

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

ZKPoSP proves bahwa public key atau address was honestly derived dari hidden wallet seed, without revealing seed atau private derivation state. Paper ini studies relationships among several derived values: apakah public keys atau addresses, possibly on different curves dan different blockchains, share hidden origin yang adalah seed atau anchor of the wallet, dan who else can recognize this relation.
