**Invisible Traces: Subversion Attacks on Batch-Issued Credentials**
Anna-Birgitta Burmeister, Anna Fennig, Andreas Franke, Karla Friedrichs, Anja Lehmann, Kurt-Kester Leißering, Konrad Letz, Cavit Özbay

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

All EU member states required to roll out digital identity system (EUDI wallet) by end of 2026. Strong privacy is core of underlying regulation yang mandates EUDI wallet to support selective disclosure dan unlinkability. Wallet currently being developed relies on batch issuance of one-time ECDSA credentials yang sign attributes through individually salted hashes. Paper ini analyzes vulnerabilities dalam approach ini.
