**Collaborative zkSNARKs with Sublinear Prover Time and Constant Proof Size**
Zhiyong Fang, Sanjam Garg, Bhaskar Roberts, Wenxuan Wu, Yupeng Zhang

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Collaborative zkSNARKs allow provers to delegate proof generation to multiple servers without compromising witness confidentiality, enabling SNARK techniques on computational limited devices. However, existing schemes have server running times as slow as single-server computation. This paper improves collaborative zkSNARK efficiency achieving sublinear prover time and constant proof size through novel server-client work distribution strategies.
