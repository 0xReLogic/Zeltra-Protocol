**Bandwidth-Efficient Robust Threshold ECDSA in Three Rounds**
Yingjie Lyu, Zengpeng Li, Hong-Sheng Zhou, Haiyang Xue, Mei Wang, Shuchao Wang, Mengling Liu

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Threshold ECDSA schemes distribute capability of issuing signatures to multiple parties, used in practical MPC wallets holding cryptocurrencies. Most prior protocols are not robust—even one misbehaving party would mandate abort. Robust schemes have been proposed but do not match state-of-the-art number of rounds which is three. Paper ini proposes robust threshold ECDSA in three rounds dengan bandwidth efficiency.
