## Summary
Digital communication increasingly underpins identity, financial transactions, and regulatory compliance. In many settings, possession of a DKIM-signed email serves as evidence of account control, transaction confirmation, or institutional affiliation. Yet demonstrating such properties typically requires revealing the full email or relying on centralized intermediaries, introducing privacy risks and additional trust assumptions.
