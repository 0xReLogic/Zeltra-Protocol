**Leakage-Resilient Circuits against NC^1, Revisited**
Yuyu Wang

**Category:** Foundations

This work revisits leakage-resilient circuits against NC¹-leakage proposing new constructions minimizing reliance on leak-free hardware. A stateless LRC scheme resilient to NC¹-leakage is presented, extended to leakage-tolerant circuits with auxiliary input. Integration with 2-adaptive leakage-resilient encoding achieves stateful LRC schemes using minimal secure hardware. The work advances practical leakage resilience with reduced hardware assumptions.
