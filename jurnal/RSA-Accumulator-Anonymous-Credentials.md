**Using the RSA or RSA-B accumulator in anonymous credential schemes**
Sietse Ringers

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Paper ini review dua RSA-based accumulators introduced by Camenisch dan Lysyanskaya in 2002 dalam setting revocation untuk anonymous credential schemes seperti Idemix atau BBS+. Show bahwa dalam such setting, lower dan upper bounds placed on accumulated values are unnecessarily strict; mereka dapat removed almost entirely (up to group order of credential scheme). This allows accumulators to be used on elliptic curves of ordinary sizes.
