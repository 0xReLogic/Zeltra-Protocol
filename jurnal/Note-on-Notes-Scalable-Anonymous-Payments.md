**A Note on Notes: Towards Scalable Anonymous Payments via Evolving Nullifiers and Oblivious Synchronization**
Sean Bowe, Ian Miers

**Category:** Applications

Anonymous payment protocols based on Zerocash have seen widespread deployment in cryptocurrencies and private smart contracts. However, a fundamental scaling limitation requires every consensus participant to maintain a perpetually growing set of nullifiers for double-spending detection. This paper addresses nullifier accumulation through evolving nullifiers and oblivious synchronization, enabling scalable anonymous payments without overwhelming storage and query requirements.
