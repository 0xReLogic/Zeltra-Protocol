**Constant-Size Inner Product Arguments for Group-Scalar Relations, Dynamic Threshold VRFs, and More**
Omid Mir, Octavio Perez-Kempner, Sebastian Ramacher, Daniel Slamanig

**Category:** Public-key cryptography

Linear algebraic relations like inner products underlie diverse cryptographic constructions including SNARKs, polynomial commitments, and VRFs. This work considers group-scalar relations ⟨A, b⟩ where A is a vector of group elements and b is a vector of field elements. Achieving constant-size arguments for such relations enables compact proofs for group operations, facilitating efficient cryptographic protocols requiring group-based computations.
