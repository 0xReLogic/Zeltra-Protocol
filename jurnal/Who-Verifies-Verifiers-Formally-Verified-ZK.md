**Who Verifies the Verifiers? Lessons Learned From Formally Verified Line-Point Zero-Knowledge**
Sabine Oechsner, Vitor Pereira, Peter Scholl

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Computer-aided cryptography with formal verification promises strong guarantees by replacing error-prone pen-and-paper proofs with machine-checked proofs. However, how reliable are these machine-checked proofs? This paper analyzes a formally verified implementation of line-point zero-knowledge, critically examining the foundations and limitations of formal verification in cryptography and providing lessons for future formally verified cryptographic systems.
