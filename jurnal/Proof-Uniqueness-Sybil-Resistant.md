**Proof-of-Uniqueness: Sybil-Resistant Privacy-Preserving Decentralized Identity through Threshold-OPRF and zk-SNARK Registry**
Adam Vozda, Martin Peresini, Juraj Mariani, Ivan Homoliak

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Several decentralized applications dan blockchains require strict one-account-per-person policy, yet public identity records often expose sensitive attributes atau enable offline attribution. Paper ini presents Proof-of-Uniqueness blockchain-based registry yang composes issuer-signed verifiable credential, two zero-knowledge proofs, threshold vOPRF, dan smart contract untuk sybil-resistant decentralized identity.
