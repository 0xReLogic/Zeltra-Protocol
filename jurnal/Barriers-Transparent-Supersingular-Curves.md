# Barriers for Transparent Algebraic Generation of Hard Supersingular Curves

**Authors:** Anis Bkakria  
**Published:** 2026-06-16  
**Category:** Foundations  

## Summary
We study transparent public generation of hard supersingular curves: a public, seeded, rerunnable algorithm outputs a supersingular curve while exposing the seed, verification transcript, and all algebraic information reconstructible from the implementation. This setting is distinct from trusted or distributed ceremonies, where a witness may be hidden, erased, or zero-knowledge protected.
