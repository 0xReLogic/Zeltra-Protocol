**Lattice-Based Linkable Ring Signatures for Anonymous and Accountable Whistleblowing**
Vishal Pareek, Aditi Kar Gangopadhyay, Sugata Gangopadhyay

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Ring signatures allow individual to sign on behalf of group such that verifier can only confirm someone in group signed it, but cannot identify actual signer. This strong anonymity, while desirable, may also be exploited for harmful activities. Linkable ring signatures mitigate this by enabling system to recognize whether two signatures originate from same signer while keeping signer anonymous. Paper ini presents lattice-based construction.
