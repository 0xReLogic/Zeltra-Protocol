# Falafel: Modular Zero-Knowledge Proofs of Training in the Federated Setting

**Authors:** Tariq Bontekoe, Sven Bootsma, Vincent Dunning, Thom Sijpesteijn, Thomas Attema  
**Published:** 2026-06-28  
**Category:** Cryptographic protocols  

## Summary
We introduce Falafel, a modular scheme for Fast, Authenticated, Locally Attested FEderated Learning, with which parties can create a zero-knowledge proof of training (zkPoT) for Federated Learning (FL). The proof guarantees active security during the federated training process as well as publicly verifiable correctness of the final, trained model.
