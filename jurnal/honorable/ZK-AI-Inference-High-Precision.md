**Zero-Knowledge AI Inference with High Precision**
Arman Riasi, Haodi Wang, Rouzbeh Behnia, Viet Vo, Thang Hoang

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Artificial Intelligence as a Service enables users to query hosted models but raises verifiability and privacy concerns. Zero-knowledge proof techniques address both concerns simultaneously but incur high proving costs due to non-linear operations. This paper presents methods for efficient ZK proofs of AI inference with high precision, enabling practical verification of machine learning computations while maintaining model and data privacy.
