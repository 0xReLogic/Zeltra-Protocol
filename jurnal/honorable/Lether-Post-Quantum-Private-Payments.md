**Lether: Practical Post-Quantum Account-Based Private Blockchain Payments**
Hongxiao Wang, Muhammed F. Esgin, Ron Steinfeld, Siu-Ming Yiu

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Lether adalah first practical account-based private blockchain payment protocol based on post-quantum lattice assumptions, following paradigm of Anonymous Zether. Main challenge lies in absence of core building blocks: unbounded-level additively-homomorphic mmPKE, dan event-oriented linkable ring signatures dengan support for multiple tags (events). Paper ini presents solutions untuk these challenges.
