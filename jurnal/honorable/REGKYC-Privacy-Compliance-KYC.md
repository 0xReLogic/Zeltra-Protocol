**REGKYC: Supporting Privacy and Compliance Enforcement for KYC in Blockchains**
Xihan Xiong, Michael Huth, William Knottenbelt

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Know Your Customer (KYC) adalah core component of Anti-Money Laundering (AML) framework designed to prevent illicit activities dalam financial systems. However, enforcing KYC dan AML on blockchains remains challenging due to difficulties establishing accountability dan preserving user privacy. REGKYC adalah privacy-preserving Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) framework yang balances user privacy dengan externally mandated KYC dan AML requirements.
