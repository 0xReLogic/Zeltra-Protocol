**AQQUA: Augmenting Quisquis with Auditability**
George Papadoulis, Danai Balla, Panagiotis Grontas, Aris Pagourtzis

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

AQQUA adalah permissionless, private, dan auditable payment system built on top of Quisquis. Berbeda dari other auditable payment systems, AQQUA supports auditing while maintaining privacy. Users bisa hold multiple accounts, perform concurrent transactions, dan features non-increasing state. AQQUA achieves auditability by introducing two authorities: one for registration dan one for auditing.
