**PriSrv: Privacy-Enhanced and Highly Usable Service Discovery in Wireless Communications**
Yang Yang, Robert H. Deng, Guomin Yang, Yingjiu Li, HweeHwa Pang, Minming Huang, Rui Shi, Jian Weng

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Service discovery essential di wireless communications. Existing service discovery protocols provide no atau very limited privacy protection untuk service providers dan clients, dan sering leak sensitive information seperti service type, client's identity dan mobility pattern. PriSrv adalah private service discovery protocol yang allows clients discover services tanpa revealing their interests atau identities.
