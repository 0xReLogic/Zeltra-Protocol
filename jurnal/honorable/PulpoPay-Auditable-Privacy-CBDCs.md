**PulpoPay for Auditable Privacy CBDCs: Introducing High Concurrency and Offline Confirmation**
Chenke Wang, Shuangcheng Liu, Yu Long, Xian Xu, Dawu Gu

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) aim to replicate utility of physical cash in digital era balancing privacy dengan regulatory auditability. Despite recent advancements, existing CBDC schemes suffer dari limited concurrency dan lack of offline payment confirmation, forcing users to process concurrent payments one by one. PulpoPay introduces high concurrency dan offline confirmation for auditable privacy CBDCs.
