## Abstract

Gradient boosted decision trees (GBDTs) are widely used for tabular data in finance, healthcare, and risk assessment. As GBDT training and inference are increasingly outsourced, clients need to verify that models are trained as claimed and predictions are consistent with those models, while providers may need to keep training data and model parameters private.
