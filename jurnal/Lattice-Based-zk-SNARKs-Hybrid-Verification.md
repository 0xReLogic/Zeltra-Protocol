**Lattice-Based zk-SNARKs with Hybrid Verification Technique**
Supriya Adhikary, Puja Mondal, Angshuman Karmakar

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Zero-knowledge SNARKs provide short privacy-preserving proofs for general NP problems with public verifiability being desirable but not always necessary. Designated-verifier zero-knowledge is useful when only specific parties should access verification results. This paper presents lattice-based zk-SNARKs with hybrid verification enabling both public and designated-verifier modalities, advancing post-quantum zero-knowledge proof schemes.
