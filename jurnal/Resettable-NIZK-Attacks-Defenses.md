# Resettable Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge: Attacks and Defenses

**Authors:** Behzad Abdolmaleki, Matteo Campanelli, Quang Dao, Shadman Mohammadi, Nahid Roustaeifar  
**Published:** 2026-05-22  
**Category:** Cryptographic protocols  

## Summary
As zero-knowledge proofs are increasingly deployed in real-world systems, they face new security threats beyond traditional theoretical guarantees. One important threat is resetting attacks, where an adversary exploits side-channel vulnerabilities or fault injection to manipulate a prover's randomness generation.
