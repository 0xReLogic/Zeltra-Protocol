**Toward a Post-Quantum Zero-Knowledge Verifiable Credential System for Self-Sovereign Identity**
Simone Dutto, Davide Margaria, Carlo Sanna, Andrea Vesco

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Advent of quantum computers brought large interest in post-quantum cryptography dan in migration to quantum-resistant systems. Protocols for Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) adalah among fundamental scenarios touched by this need. Core concept of SSI adalah move control of digital identity dari third-party identity providers directly to individuals melalui Verificable Credentials (VCs) supporting anonymity dan selective disclosure.
