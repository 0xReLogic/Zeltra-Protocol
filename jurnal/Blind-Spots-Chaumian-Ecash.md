**Blind Spots in Blind Signatures: A System-Level Security Analysis of Deployed Chaumian Ecash**
Huaifeng Chen, Yuchang Zhang, Yu Cheng

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Cashu, dominant deployed Chaumian ecash system, has grown to 31 specification documents, 58 indexed mints, dan seven interoperable wallets, while cryptographic literature has studied only its blind-signature core. Every incident in system's short audited history, including cross-mint theft disclosed in early 2026, is compositional: emerges from keyset rotation, wallet recovery, dan multi-mint identity. Paper ini gives first system-level security analysis.
