**AVPEU: Anonymous Verifiable Presentations with Extended Usability**
Yalan Wang, Liqun Chen, Yangguang Tian, Long Meng, Christopher J.P. Newton

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

W3C has established standards for decentralized identities (DIDs) and verifiable credentials (VCs). Users generate verifiable presentations (VPs) proving credential ownership. W3C standards advocate randomizable signatures for VC creation and zero-knowledge proofs for VP generation. AVPEU presents anonymous verifiable presentations with extended usability, implementing W3C standards while enhancing privacy and enabling practical deployment of W3C-compliant identity systems.
