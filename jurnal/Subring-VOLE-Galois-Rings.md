**Authors:** Ignacio Cascudo, Xiang Liu  
**Published:** 2026-09-02  
**Category:** Cryptographic protocols  

## Summary
Vector oblivious linear evaluation (VOLE) is a type of correlation that is widely used in multiparty computation (MPC) and zero-knowledge (ZK) proofs. Recently, the generation of VOLE correlation has become very efficient due to the pseudorandom correlation generator (PCGs) paradigm (Boyle et al. CCS 2018) and SoftSpokenOT (Roy Crypto 2022).
