**P2P Mixing and Unlinkable Bitcoin Transactions**
Tim Ruffing, Pedro Moreno-Sanchez, Aniket Kate

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Starting with Dining Cryptographers networks (DC-net), several peer-to-peer (P2P) anonymous communication protocols have been proposed. Despite strong anonymity guarantees none employed in practice: Most fail to simultaneously handle crucial problems of slot collisions dan malicious peers, while remaining ones handle those dengan significant increased latency linear in number of participating peers.
