**Concurrently secure variants of blind (Okamoto-)Schnorr signatures**
Georg Fuchsbauer, Fabian Regen, Levente Sulyok

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci-Publik

Paper ini presents variants of blind Schnorr dan blind Okamoto-Schnorr signatures yang tidak susceptible to ROS-style attacks. Prove schemes unforgeable dalam algebraic group model dan random oracle model: Okamoto-Schnorr variant under discrete-logarithm assumption; Schnorr variant under algebraic one-more discrete-logarithm assumption. Schnorr variant improves on communication complexity of Snowblind (CRYPTO 2023), state-of-the-art pairing-free blind signatures.
