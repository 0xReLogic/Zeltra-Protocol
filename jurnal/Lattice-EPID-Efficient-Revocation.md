**Lattice EPID with Efficient Revocation**
Corentin Jeudy, Olivier Sanders

**Category:** Public-key cryptography

Enhanced Privacy Identification (EPID) is deployed in billions of chips and standardized at ISO. EPID's decentralized revocation allows revoking signers by placing signatures on a revocation list. Each new signature must include proof it used a different key. This paper presents lattice-based EPID with efficient revocation enabling post-quantum anonymous authentication with scalable revocation mechanisms.
