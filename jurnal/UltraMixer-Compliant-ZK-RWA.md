**UltraMixer: A Compliant Zero-Knowledge Privacy Layer for Tokenized Real-World Assets**
Zonglun Li, Hong Kang, Xue Liu

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Real-world-asset (RWA) tokens memberikan fractional ownership dan continuous settlement, tetapi recording claims di transparent public ledgers exposes flows dan positions, undermining market confidentiality. UltraMixer adalah noncustodial privacy layer natively compatible dengan ERC-3643. Compliance enforced at boundary via zero-knowledge proofs, enabling privacy dengan enforceable regulatory access control.
