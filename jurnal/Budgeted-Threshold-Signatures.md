**Budgeted Threshold Signatures: Protocol-Native Spending Limits with Zero On-Chain Footprint**
Huaifeng Chen, Yuchang Zhang, Yu Cheng

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Spending limits adalah workhorse of custody policy, yet in every deployed threshold-signing stack they live outside signing protocol—in centralized policy engines, on-chain covenants, atau hardware co-signers—where they leak budget state, bind to single chain, atau add trusted bypass surface. Workload is compounding: agent payments passed one hundred million transfers on single chain in under a year. Paper ini initiates study of protocol-native spending limits.
