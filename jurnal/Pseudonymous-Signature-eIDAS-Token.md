**Towards Practical Security of Pseudonymous Signature on the BSI eIDAS Token**
Mirosław Kutyłowski, Lucjan Hanzlik, Kamil Kluczniak

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Paper ini presents extension of Pseudonymous Signature introduced by German Federal BSI authority as part of technical recommendations for electronic identity documents. Without switching to pairing friendly groups, enhance scheme so that issuer does not know private keys of citizen (cannot impersonate citizen), dan powerful adversary that breaks any number of ID cards cannot forge new cards yang could be proven as legitimate.
