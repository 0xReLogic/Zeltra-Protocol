**Untagging Tor: A Formal Treatment of Onion Encryption**
Jean Paul Degabriele, Martijn Stam

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Tor adalah primary tool untuk maintaining anonymity online, providing low-latency, circuit-based, bidirectional secure channel through network of onion routers, obscuring who is talking to whom even to adversaries controlling part of network. Tor relies heavily on cryptographic techniques, yet onion encryption scheme susceptible to tagging attacks (Fu dan Ling, 2009) allowing active adversary controlling first dan last node untuk break anonymity.
