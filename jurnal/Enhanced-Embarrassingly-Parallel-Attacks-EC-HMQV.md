**Enhanced Embarrassingly Parallel Attacks against EC-HMQV-C and ECMQV**
Augustin P. SARR

**Kategori:** Serangan dan Kriptanalisis

Sarr introduced embarrassingly parallel impersonation attack against ECMQV dan EC-HMQV-C whose core step is adding-based pseudo-random walk over cyclic group G, of order n, searching for decomposed i-point. Paper ini revisits construction in richer multi-party setting where two sets of parties, initiators dan responders, interact.
