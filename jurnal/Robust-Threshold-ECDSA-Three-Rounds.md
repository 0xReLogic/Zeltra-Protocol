**Robust Threshold ECDSA with Online-Friendly Design in Three Rounds**
Guofeng Tang, Haiyang Xue

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Threshold signatures especially ECDSA enhance key protection dengan addressing single-point-of-failure issue. Threshold signing dapat dibagi menjadi offline dan online phases berdasarkan apakah message required. Schemes dengan low-cost online phases disebut online-friendly. Robustness critical untuk real-world applications, guarantees successful completion whenever threshold number t of semi-honest parties participates.
