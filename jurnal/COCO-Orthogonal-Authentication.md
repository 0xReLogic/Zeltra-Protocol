**COCO: Coconuts and Oblivious Computations for Orthogonal Authentication**
Yamya Reiki

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Authentication often bridges real-world individuals dan their virtual public identities seperti usernames, user IDs dan e-mails, exposing vulnerabilities yang threaten user privacy. COCO adalah framework yang segregates roles among Verifiers, Authenticators, dan Clients untuk achieve privacy-preserving authentication. COCO eliminates need untuk Authenticators to directly access virtual public identifiers.
