**OpenAC: Open Design for Transparent and Lightweight Anonymous Credentials**
Liam Eagen, Hy Ngo, Vikas Rushi, Ying Tong, Moven Tsai, Janabel Xia

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Sistem identitas digital memerlukan mekanisme untuk verifiable, privacy-preserving presentations of user attestations. Pendekatan trivial menggunakan selective disclosure by presenting individually signed attestations introduces persistent linkability yang compromises anonymity. Existing anonymous credential systems memiliki practical drawbacks: beberapa depend pada trusted setups, lainnya require substantial modifications ke issuer's issuance flow.
