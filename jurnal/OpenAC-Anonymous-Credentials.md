**OpenAC: Open Design for Transparent and Lightweight Anonymous Credentials**
Liam Eagen, Hy Ngo, Vikas Rushi, Ying Tong, Moven Tsai, Janabel Xia

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Digital identity systems require mechanisms for verifiable, privacy-presenting presentations of user attestations. Trivial approach utilizing selective disclosure introduces persistent linkability compromising user anonymity. Existing anonymous credential systems have practical drawbacks: some depend on trusted setups, others require substantial modifications ke issuer's issuance flow. OpenAC proposes open design untuk lightweight anonymous credentials.
