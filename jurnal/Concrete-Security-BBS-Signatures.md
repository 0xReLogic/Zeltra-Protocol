**On the Concrete Security of BBS/BBS+ Signatures**
Rutchathon Chairattana-Apirom, Stefano Tessaro

**Kategori:** Serangan dan Kriptanalisis

BBS/BBS+ signatures adalah most promising solution untuk practical dan lightweight anonymous credentials, underlies standardization efforts by W3C dan IRTF. Due to potential for large scale deployment, paramount untuk understand concrete security. Paper ini analyzes security proofs dari Au et al., Camenisch et al., dan Tessaro dan Zhu, showing reductions dari q-SDH dan q-Strong Diffie-Hellman assumptions.
