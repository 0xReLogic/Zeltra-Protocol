**Always by Your Side: Constructing Traceable Anonymous Credentials with Hardware-Binding**
Chang Chen, Guoyu Yang, Qi Chen, Wei Wang, Jin Li

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

With development of decentralized identity (DID), anonymous credential (AC) technology dan its traceability receiving more attention. Most works introduce trusted party (regulator) yang holds decryption key atau backdoor untuk directly deanonymize user identity. Paper ini constructs traceable anonymous credentials with hardware-binding untuk handle complex tracing tasks among large amounts of user profiles stored by issuer dan authentication records.
