**Nonce- and Redundancy-encrypting Modes with Farfalle**
Seth Hoffert

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci-Secara

Nonces are a fact of life untuk achieving semantic security. Generating uniformly random nonce can be costly dan may not always be feasible. Using anything other than uniformly random bits can result in information leakage; e.g., timestamp can deanonymize communication dan counter can leak quantity of transmitted messages. Ideally, efficiently encrypt nonce to avoid needing uniformly random bits dan avoid information leakage.
