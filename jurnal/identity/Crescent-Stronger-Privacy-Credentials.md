**Crescent: Stronger Privacy for Existing Credentials**
Christian Paquin, Guru-Vamsi Policharla, Greg Zaverucha

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Crescent adalah construction dan implementation of privacy-preserving credentials yang upgrades privacy features of existing credentials seperti JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) dan Mobile Driver's License (mDL) tanpa requiring new party to issue credentials. Menggunakan zero-knowledge proofs of possession of these credentials, privacy features seperti selective disclosure dan unlinkability bisa ditambahkan tanpa help dari credential issuers.
