**Threshold Anonymous Credentials with Silent Setup**
Preshtha Garg, Sanjam Garg, Guru-Vamsi Policharla, Bhaskar Roberts

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Anonymous credentials allow users to authenticate anonymously dan unlinkably. By end of 2026, EU member states will be required to issue digital identity wallets enabling authentication in this manner. In decentralized settings, we desire schemes dengan additional properties: schemes yang allow multiple authorities to issue credentials, hide identities of issuers, dan allow verifiers to dynamically choose policies. Paper ini presents such scheme.
