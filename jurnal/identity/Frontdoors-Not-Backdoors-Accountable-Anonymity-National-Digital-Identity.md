## Abstract

Anonymous credentials (ACs) let users prove identity claims with strong privacy guarantees, and national systems built on them - EUDI and the Swiss e-ID - go live soon. Due to these guarantees, however, AC systems cannot detect proof proxy attacks, where malicious users "lend" their identity by presenting their credentials on behalf of other users, exposing a fundamental gap between privacy and accountability.
