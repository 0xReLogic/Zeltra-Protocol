**DIMSEPP: A Decentralized Identity Management System with Enhanced Privacy Protection**
Yu Zhang, Zongbin Wang

**Category:** Applications

DIMSEPP proposes a decentralized identity management system enhancing privacy while preserving blockchain verifiability. The system cryptographically enforces data minimal disclosure by storing attribute commitments on-chain and validating through zero-knowledge proofs, allowing users to demonstrate attribute validity without revealing sensitive values. The architecture maintains compatibility with existing DID standards through standard document representations.
