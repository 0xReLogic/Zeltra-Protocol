**Vega: Low-Latency Zero-Knowledge Proofs over Existing Credentials**
Darya Kaviani, Srinath Setty

**Category:** Applications

As digital identity verification becomes pervasive, existing privacy-preserving approaches suffer from complex circuit designs, large proof sizes, trusted setups, or high latency. Vega is a practical zero-knowledge proof system proving statements about existing credentials without revealing other information. It is simple, requires no trusted setup, and achieves superior efficiency: for 1920-byte credentials, Vega achieves 92ms proving time.
