**Anonymous Attestation Using the Strong Diffie Hellman Assumption Revisited**
Jan Camenisch, Manu Drijvers, Anja Lehmann

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Direct Anonymous Attestation (DAA) adalah cryptographic protocol untuk privacy-protecting authentication, standardized in TPM standard dan implemented in millions of chips. Variant of DAA juga used in Intel's SGX. Recently, Camenisch et al. demonstrated bahwa existing security models for DAA do not correctly capture all security requirements, dan showed number of flaws in existing schemes based on LRSW assumption. Paper ini identify flaws dalam security proofs.
