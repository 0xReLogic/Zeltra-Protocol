**Universally Composable Auditable Surveillance**
Valerie Fetzer, Michael Klooß, Jörn Müller-Quade, Markus Raiber, Andy Rupp

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

User privacy becoming increasingly important in digital society. Yet many applications face legal requirements atau regulations yang prohibit unconditional anonymity guarantees, e.g., electronic payments dimana surveillance mandated untuk investigate suspected crimes. Many systems have no effective privacy protections at all, atau have backdoors stored at operator side yang can be used by authorities untuk disclose private information. Paper ini addresses auditable surveillance dengan UC framework.
