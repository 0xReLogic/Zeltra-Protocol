**iToken: One-Time-Use Anonymous Token with Issuance Hiding**
Zengpeng Li, Xiangyu Su, Dongfang Wei, Guangyu Liao, Mei Wang

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Privacy-Enhancing KYC integrates one-time-use anonymous tokens (OTATs) into self-sovereign identity frameworks seperti EUDI Wallet, Apple's Private Access Tokens, dan W3C's Privacy-Preserving Advertising proposals, to enable regulatory compliance while preserving user anonymity. Paper ini designs new OTAT scheme to mitigate targeted DoS attacks dan prevent token misuse seperti farming dan replay.
