**How to Bind Anonymous Credentials to Humans**
Julia Hesse, Nitin Singh, Alessandro Sorniotti

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Digital dan paper-based authentication adalah two predominant mechanisms deployed untuk authenticate end-users. Ketika verification of digital credential dilakukan in person (misalnya authentication required to access facilities during COVID pandemic), kedua mechanisms often deployed together: verifier checks government-issued ID to match picture to individual, lalu checks digital credential on device. Paper ini shows bagaimana bind anonymous credentials to humans.
