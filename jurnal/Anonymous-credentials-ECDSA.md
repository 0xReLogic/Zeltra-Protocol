**Anonymous credentials from ECDSA**
Matteo Frigo, abhi shelat

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Anonymous digital credentials allow user to prove possession of attribute asserted by identity issuer tanpa revealing extra information about themselves. Misalnya, user dengan digital passport credential can prove "age > 18" tanpa revealing name atau date of birth. Despite clear application, anonymous credential schemes telah difficult to realize menggunakan ECDSA due to algebraic structure dan lack of suitable primitives.
