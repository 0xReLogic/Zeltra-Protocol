**Quasar: Sublinear Multi-Cast Commitment Mixing in Recursive Accumulation**
Tianyu Zheng, Shang Gao, Sherman S. M. Chow, Yu Guo, Bin Xiao

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Folding-based recursion cores on multi-instance accumulation requiring recursive verifiers to combine ℓ committed objects with Θ(ℓ) circuit-expensive commitment random linear combinations (CRCs). Eliminating this linear overhead is crucial for scalability. Quasar achieves sublinear multi-cast commitment mixing through novel compression techniques, enabling significantly more efficient recursive accumulation for verifiable computation and scaling folding-based proofs.
