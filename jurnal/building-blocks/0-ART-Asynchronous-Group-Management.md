**0-ART: Asynchronous and Verifiable Group Management for Decentralized Applications**
Yevhen Hrubiian, Illia Melnyk, Volodymyr Dubinin, Oleksandr Kurbatov, Serhii Volynets, Roman Perebynos, Yevhenii Serdiukov

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Modern e2e private applications face scalability issues limiting functionality and adoption. Some organize groups as peer-to-peer chats (quadratic communication complexity), while others use scalable constructions like ART but lack verification support. This paper presents 0-ART, an asynchronous and verifiable group management protocol for decentralized applications achieving both scalability and end-to-end verifiability of group operations.
