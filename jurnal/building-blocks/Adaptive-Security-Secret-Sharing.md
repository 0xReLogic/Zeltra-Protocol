**How to Make Any Computational Secret Sharing Scheme Adaptively Secure**
George Lu, Brent Waters

**Kategori:** Fondasi

Secret sharing (SS) adalah foundational cryptographic primitive dengan diverse applications termasuk secure multiparty computation dan conditional disclosure of secrets. Recent advancements increasingly leverage computational assumptions untuk achieve more efficient constructions dan support broader access policies. Paper ini menunjukkan bagaimana membuat any computational secret sharing (CSS) scheme adaptively secure.
