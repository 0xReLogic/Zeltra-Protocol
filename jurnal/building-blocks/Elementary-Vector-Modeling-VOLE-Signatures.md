# Elementary-Vector Modeling for Shorter VOLE-Based Signatures from SD and PKP

**Authors:** Boyuan Gao, Zongyang Zhang, Weihan Li, Jianwei Liu, Jianting Ning  
**Published:** 2026-08-18  
**Category:** Public-key cryptography  

## Summary
Zero-knowledge proofs based on VOLE have recently become a promising approach for designing post-quantum signature schemes. Such schemes are constructed by having a signer prove knowledge of a secret input for a prescribed one-way function. In this work, we optimize VOLE-based signature schemes from syndrome decoding (SD) and the permuted kernel problem (PKP).
