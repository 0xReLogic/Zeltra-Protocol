**Lattice-Based Group Signatures in the Standard Model, Revisited**
Nam Tran, Khoa Nguyen, Dongxi Liu, Josef Pieprzyk, Willy Susilo

**Category:** Public-key cryptography

Lattice-based group signatures have been researched since 2010 with recent advances in random oracle models achieving strong security and practical efficiency. However, the standard model state-of-affairs remains less satisfactory. Existing schemes from Katsumata-Yamada or generic NIZK constructions are inefficient. This paper advances lattice-based group signatures in the standard model, achieving post-quantum security with improved practical performance.
