# The Small-Field Turn in Succinct Proofs: A Systematization of Finite-Field Choice in Modern SNARKs and STARKs

**Authors:** Christos Skatharoudis  
**Published:** 2026-07-09  
**Category:** Cryptographic protocols  

## Summary
Over the past half-decade, transparent succinct arguments have migrated off the 256-bit scalar fields of pairing-friendly elliptic curves and onto small fields: the 64-bit Goldilocks prime, the 31-bit primes BabyBear and KoalaBear, the Mersenne prime 2³¹−1 reached through the circle construction, and binary tower fields down to F₂.
