## Abstract

We present Bulletproofs* (BP*, BulletproofsStar), a folding scheme for arithmetic circuit proofs under standard assumptions and without preprocessing, i.e., for the arithmetic circuit satisfiability language of Bulletproofs (S&P 18), following the recipe of ProtoStar (ePrint 2023/620). To this end, we first adapt the algebraic verifiers of the arithmetic circuit proof of Bulletproofs to the algebraic form required by ProtoStar, and prove that the modified protocol remains secure.
