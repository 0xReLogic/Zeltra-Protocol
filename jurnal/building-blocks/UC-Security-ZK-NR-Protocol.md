**UC-Security of the ZK-NR Protocol under Contextual Entropy Constraints: A Composable Zero-Knowledge Attestation Framework**
MINKA MI NGUIDJOI Thierry Emmanuel

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

The CRO Trilemma formalizes the inherent incompatibility between confidentiality, reliability, and legal opposability in proof systems. This paper provides complete Universal Composability (UC) security proofs for ZK-NR protocol, a layered architecture approaching this bound. Modeling each dialectical layer (Iron, Gold, Clay) as ideal functionality with erasure semantics and proving indistinguishability under post-quantum assumptions.
