**Reed-Solomon Codes Beyond Johnson: Efficient Decoding and Smaller Cryptographic Proofs**
Quang Dao, Scott Duke Kominers, Justin Thaler

**Kategori:** Fondasi

List decoding Reed-Solomon codes adalah central problem in coding theory dan, together dengan mutual correlated agreement (MCA), underpins soundness of many succinct cryptographic proofs. Paper ini gives precise quantitative bounds on Reed-Solomon list decoding dan MCA up to capacity, provides deterministic decoding algorithms efficient over cryptographically large fields, dan specializes bounds to reduce proof sizes.
