## Abstract

The classical results of Cramer et al. [Crypto'94] showed how to compose n Σ-protocols with statistical HVZK to obtain an efficient proof of knowledge of their disjunction maintaining statistical HVZK without adding hardness assumptions. The Fiat-Shamir (FS) transform applied to their construction produces a statistical NIZK proof of knowledge in the random oracle (RO) model, but, unfortunately, the proof size in their case is linear in n.
