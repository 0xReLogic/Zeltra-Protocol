**Non-Interactive Threshold BBS+ From Pseudorandom Correlations**
Sebastian Faust, Carmit Hazay, David Kretzler, Leandro Rometsch, Benjamin Schlosser

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci Publik

BBS+ signature scheme adalah most prominent solution untuk realizing anonymous credentials dengan properties seperti selective disclosure dan efficient protocols. Traditionally, single credential issuer produces BBS+ signatures yang poses significant risks due to single point of failure. Paper ini addresses threat via novel t-out-of-n threshold BBS+ protocol supporting non-interactive key generation.
