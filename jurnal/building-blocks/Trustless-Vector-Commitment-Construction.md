**Trustless Delegation of Vector Commitment Construction in Resource-Constrained Settings**
Parisa Hassanizadeh, Shahriar Ebrahimi, Stefan Dziembowski, Janusz Szczepanski

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Sequential data types (video, audio) require both integrity and order preservation. Verifiers often access only partial sequences due to privacy or bandwidth constraints motivating vector commitments for verifiable partial disclosure. However, VC construction requires trusted committers on secure modules, challenging for resource-constrained devices. This paper presents trustless delegation enabling resource-limited devices to participate in vector commitment schemes without trusted modules.
