**Foundations of Adaptor Signatures**
Paul Gerhart, Dominique Schröder, Pratik Soni, Sri AravindaKrishnan Thyagarajan

**Kategori:** Aplikasi

Adaptor signatures extend functionality of regular signatures melalui computation of pre-signatures on messages untuk statements of NP relations. Pre-signatures publicly verifiable; simultaneously hide dan commit to signature of underlying signature scheme on that message. Anybody possessing corresponding witness dapat adapt pre-signature to obtain "regular" signature. Adaptor signatures have found numerous applications untuk conditional payments.
