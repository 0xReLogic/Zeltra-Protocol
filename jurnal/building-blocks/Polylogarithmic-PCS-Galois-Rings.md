**Polylogarithmic Polynomial Commitment Scheme over Galois Rings**
Zhuo Wu, Xinxuan Zhang, Yi Deng, Yuanju Wei, Zhongliang Zhang, Liuyu Yang

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

This paper introduces the first multilinear polynomial commitment scheme (PCS) over Galois rings achieving O(log²n) verification cost with O(n log n) committing time and O(n) evaluation opening prover time. This PCS enables zero-knowledge proofs for arithmetic circuits over Galois rings, facilitating verifiable computation in applications requiring proofs of polynomial ring operations such as verifiable fully homomorphic encryption.
