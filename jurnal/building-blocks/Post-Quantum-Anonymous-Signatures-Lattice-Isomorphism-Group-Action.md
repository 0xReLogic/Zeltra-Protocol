**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Abstract

Post-quantum assumptions may not rely on the difficulty of finding secret subgroups as many classical schemes did. Instead, several assumptions make use of more general group actions, with the hope that quantum algorithms are not helpful in this less structured setting.
