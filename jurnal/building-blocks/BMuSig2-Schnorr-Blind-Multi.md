**BMuSig2: Schnorr-Compatible Blind Multi-Signatures**
Kanchan Bisht, Keerthi Aiswarya Varshini, Shivam Sethi, Maria Francis, R. Kabaleeshwaran

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Blind signatures dan multi-signatures are well-known primitives, but blind multi-signatures (BMS) recently formalized by Karantaidou et al (CCS'24). BMS scheme allows user to obtain compact signature on common hidden message dari group of signers such that even if signers collude, they cannot learn message atau link signature to particular interaction. BMuSig2 introduces 2-round concurrently secure BMS scheme.
