**Blind ECDSA from the ECDSA Assumption**
Jules Maire, Alan Pulval-Dady

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Blind signatures are cornerstones for privacy-sensitive applications including digital cash, anonymous credentials, and electronic voting. ECDSA is widely adopted for efficiency in resource-constrained environments like mobile devices and blockchains. This paper presents the first blind ECDSA construction relying solely on the ECDSA assumption, overcoming inherent challenges in blinding elliptic curve operations while maintaining practical efficiency.
