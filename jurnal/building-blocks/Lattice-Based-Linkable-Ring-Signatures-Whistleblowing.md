**Lattice-Based Linkable Ring Signatures for Anonymous and Accountable Whistleblowing**
Vishal Pareek, Aditi Kar Gangopadhyay, Sugata Gangopadhyay

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Ring signatures enable individuals to sign on behalf of a group while hiding their identity. However, this strong anonymity can be exploited for harmful activities. Linkable ring signatures mitigate this by enabling the system to recognize whether two signatures originate from the same signer while maintaining anonymity. This paper presents lattice-based linkable ring signatures suitable for post-quantum whistleblowing systems that provide accountability without compromising signer privacy.
