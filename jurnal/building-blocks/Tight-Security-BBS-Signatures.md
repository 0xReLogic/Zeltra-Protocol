**Tight Security for BBS Signatures**
Rutchathon Chairattana-Apirom, Dennis Hofheinz, Stefano Tessaro

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci Publik

Paper ini study concrete security of BBS signatures (Boneh, Boyen, Shacham, CRYPTO '04; Camenisch dan Lysyanskaya, CRYPTO '04), popular algebraic construction yang underlies practical privacy-preserving authentication systems dan undergoing standardization by W3C dan IRTF. Schäge memberikan tight standard-model security proof under q-SDH assumption untuk variant BBS+ yang less efficient.
