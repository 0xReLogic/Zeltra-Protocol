## Abstract

An adaptor signature scheme can be seen as committing to a signature using an NP statement, in such a way that (1) the commitment, called a presignature, is verifiable, (2) the corresponding witness enables opening the commitment (adapting the presignature), and (3) seeing a valid commitment-opening (presignature-signature) pair leaks the witness.
