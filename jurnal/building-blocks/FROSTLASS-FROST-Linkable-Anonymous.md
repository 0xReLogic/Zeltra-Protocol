**FROSTLASS: Flexible Ring-Oriented Schnorr-like Thresholdized Linkably Anonymous Signature Scheme**
Joshua Babb, Brandon Goodell, Rigo Salazar, Freeman Slaughter, Luke Szramowski

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

FROST adalah pragmatic method of thresholdizing Schnorr signatures, permitting threshold quorum of t signers out of n total individuals to sign for a message. Scheme improved state of art, resulting in efficient protocol yang aborts in presence of up to t-1 malicious users dengan strong resilience against chosen-message attacks. Paper ini builds upon FROST presenting FROSTLASS dengan enhanced anonymity properties.
