# A Toolkit for Succinct Lattice-Based Zero Knowledge Proofs

**Authors:** Beatrice Biasioli, Madalina Bolboceanu, Vadim Lyubashevsky, Antonio Merino-Gallardo, Michał Osadnik, Gregor Seiler, Patrick Steuer  
**Published:** 2026-06-18  
**Category:** Cryptographic protocols  

## Summary
The development of proof systems whose security relies on the hardness of lattice problems has been a fruitful research area in recent years. By leveraging the techniques introduced in LaBRADOR (Beullens, Seiler, Crypto 2023), the state-of-the-art lattice-based schemes have very fast provers and have output sizes under 100KB for arbitrarily large statements.
