**Turning Simulation into Construction: New Uses of NIZK Simulators**
Stephan Krenn, Kai Samelin, Daniel Slamanig

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Simulation is a fundamental technique in cryptography central to security proofs and zero-knowledge demonstration. This paper considers using simulators of non-interactive zero-knowledge proofs (NIZKs) not merely as proof techniques but as integral components of cryptographic designs. Applications include designated verifier signatures, deniable encryption, and recent privacy-preserving constructions, showing that NIZK simulators enable novel cryptographic functionalities beyond their traditional role.
