**WabiSabi: Centrally Coordinated CoinJoins with Variable Amounts**
Ádám Ficsór, Yuval Kogman, Lucas Ontivero, István András Seres

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Bitcoin transfers value on public ledger anyone can verify. Coin ownership defined in terms of public keys. Despite potential use for private transfers, research shows users' activity can often be traced in practice. Businesses built on dragnet surveillance of Bitcoin users karena lack of strong privacy, harming fungibility, basic property of functional money. WabiSabi provides centrally coordinated CoinJoins with variable amounts.
