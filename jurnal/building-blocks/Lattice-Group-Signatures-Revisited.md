## Abstract

Group signatures are one of the central privacy-preserving authentication mechanisms, offering an interesting trade-off between accountability and anonymity. Their versatility has led to many applications and even standardization at ISO/IEC. Unfortunately, they lack so far efficient quantum-safe constructions, despite several works implementing the seminal framework by Bellare, Micciancio and Warinschi (BMW) in the lattice setting.
