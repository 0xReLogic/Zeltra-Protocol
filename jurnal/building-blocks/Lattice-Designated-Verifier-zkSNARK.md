**A Lattice-based Designated Verifier zkSNARK from Standard Assumptions**
Mohammad Sadegh Ahmadi, Taraneh Eghlidos, Behzad Abdolmaleki, Ngoc Khanh Nguyen

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Designated Verifier zero-knowledge SNARKs (DV-zkSNARKs) restrict proof verification ability to authorized parties only, unlike publicly verifiable SNARKs. Existing lattice-based constructions rely on linear-only encryption or Linear Targeted Malleability. This paper presents a lattice-based DV-zkSNARK from standard assumptions, advancing post-quantum cryptography for scenarios requiring restricted verifiability with strong privacy guarantees.
