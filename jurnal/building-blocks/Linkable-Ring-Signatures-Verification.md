**Revisiting Linkable Ring Signatures with Logarithmic Verification Complexity**
Danai Balla, Pyrros Chaidos

**Category:** Attacks and cryptanalysis

Ring signatures enable signing on behalf of ad-hoc public key sets while hiding signer identity. Linkable ring signatures detect signatures from the same signer while maintaining anonymity. The LLRing family by Hui and Chau is one of the more efficient LRS schemes. This paper analyzes LLRing finding unlinkability vulnerabilities and proposing improvements for secure anonymous transaction and e-voting applications.
