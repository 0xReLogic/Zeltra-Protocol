**Identity-Based Revocable and Linkable Ring Signature**
Muyuan Wang

**Kategori:** Kriptografi Kunci-Publik

Revocable and linkable ring signatures (RLRS) provide practical mechanism for controllable anonymity, enabling revocation authority (RA) untuk mandatorily revoke anonymity of real signer. However, existing constructions often rely on assumption of fully trusted RA, where correctness of revocation is not publicly verifiable rendering honest users vulnerable to undetected framing by compromised RA. Paper ini addresses these limitations.
