# Failure Is Not Silent: Attacks on Blind Signatures

**Authors:** Maksymilian Gorski, Lucjan Hanzlik  
**Published:** 2026-09-15  
**Category:** Attacks and cryptanalysis  

## Summary
Blind signatures and OPRFs are widely used to build anonymous tokens and privacy-preserving protocols. This paper shows that several prior claims of blindness and request-privacy for such primitives are false under the standard security definitions. In particular, we give explicit malicious-signer/evaluator adversaries showing that the theorems claimed for several existing constructions, including the blind ECDSA scheme of Qin et al. and the verifiable Dark Matter OPRF, do not hold.
