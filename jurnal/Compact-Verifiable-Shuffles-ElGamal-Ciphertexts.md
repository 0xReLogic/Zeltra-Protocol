## Abstract

A verifiable shuffle proves that output ciphertexts are a rerandomized permutation of the inputs without revealing the permutation or rerandomization factors. It is a core primitive in mix-nets for electronic voting and blockchain-based anonymization, where each mix server publishes an auditable proof.
