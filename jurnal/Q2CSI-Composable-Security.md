**Quantum Composable and Contextual Security Infrastructure (Q2CSI): A Modular Architecture for Legally Explainable Cryptographic Signatures**
Thierry Emmanuel MINKA MI NGUIDJOI, MANI ONANA Flavien Serge, DJOTIO NDIÉ Thomas, BOUETOU BOUETOU Thomas

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

The CRO trilemma formalizes fundamental incompatibility between confidentiality, reliability, and legal opposability imposing an entropic bound on cryptographic security in contextual adversarial settings. Q2CSI introduces a layered framework resolving this trilemma through dialectical separation into three composable layers: Iron (reliability), Gold (confidentiality), Clay (legal opposability), advancing cryptography for legally compliant systems.
