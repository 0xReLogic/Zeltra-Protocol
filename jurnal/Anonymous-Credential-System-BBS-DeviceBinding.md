**A Specification of an Anonymous Credential System Using BBS+ Signatures with Privacy-Preserving Revocation and Device Binding**
Christoph Graebnitz, Nicolas Buchmann, Martin Seiffert, Marian Margraf

**Kategori:** Protokol Kriptografi

Growing interest in anonymous credentials (ACs) karena mereka can mitigate risk of personal data being processed by untrusted actors tanpa consent. Privacy-by-design paradigm memungkinkan users prove possession of personal attributes seperti government document containing sensitive personal information, while preserving privacy. Paper ini specifies anonymous credential system using BBS+ signatures dengan privacy-preserving revocation dan device binding.
