**Design ZK-NR: A Post-Quantum Layered Protocol for Legally Explainable Zero-Knowledge Non-Repudiation Attestation**
Minka Mi Nguidjoi Thierry Emmanuel, Mani Onana Flavien Serge, Djotio Ndié Thomas, Atsa Etoundi Roger

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

This article presents the architectural design of Zero Knowledge Non-Repudiation (ZK-NR), a layered cryptographic protocol for post-quantum secure, legally interpretable, and verifiably non-repudiable attestations. Built on STARK-based zero-knowledge proofs, hybrid post-quantum signatures, and entropy-accumulating ledger anchoring, ZK-NR satisfies Q2CSI framework and NIZK-E model properties while achieving semantic interpretability for legal compliance.
