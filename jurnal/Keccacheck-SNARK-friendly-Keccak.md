**Keccacheck: towards a SNARK friendly Keccak**
Marcin Kostrzewa, Matthew Klein, Ara Adkins, Grzegorz Świrski, Wojciech Żmuda

**Category:** Cryptographic protocols

Keccak, the hash function at Ethereum's core, is computationally expensive in SNARK circuits, creating a critical bottleneck for ZK reasoning about blockchain state. Recent state-of-the-art relies on lookup arguments achieving better performance than direct proving but still requires tens of thousands of constraints. Keccacheck optimizes SNARK-friendly Keccak proving, enabling more efficient on-chain verification and ZK applications involving hash computations.
