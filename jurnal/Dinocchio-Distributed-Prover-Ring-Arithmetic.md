## Abstract

Nearly all existing SNARK systems are optimized for arithmetic over finite fields. Using them to prove statements involving ring arithmetic, which underlies lattice-based cryptography and fully homomorphic encryption (FHE), incurs substantial overhead. Consequently, practical deployments of FHE often rely on the honest-but-curious assumptions, leaving a gap in the verifiability of the FHE computation.
