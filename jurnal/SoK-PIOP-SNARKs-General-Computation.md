# SoK: PIOP-based SNARKs for General Computation

**Authors:** Yonghui Guan, Rihe Zhang, Bin Liu, Tianyu Zhao, Jialu Hao, Antonis Michalas  
**Published:** 2026-05-31  
**Category:** Foundations  

## Summary
Many modern SNARK constructions follow a paradigm that combines a Polynomial Interactive Oracle Proof (PIOP) with an appropriate Polynomial Commitment Scheme (PCS). In this paradigm, the PIOP reduces soundness to the verification of a collection of polynomial relations that are checked though oracle queries, while the PCS enables succinct commitments to the corresponding polynomials.
