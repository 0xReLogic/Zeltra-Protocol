## Summary
Large Language Models (LLMs) are frontier deep learning systems that have achieved remarkable success across a wide range of AI services. However, their substantial computational and memory requirements make them difficult to deploy and run on local hardware. Due to these resource requirements, users often rely on untrusted cloud infrastructure providers to perform model inference.
